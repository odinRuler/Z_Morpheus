## Scouting Workflow

### When asked "check for new offers" or "scan job sites":
1. **Always check Tier 1 first** — LinkedIn + eFinancialCareors are the highest yield.
2. Use `web_search` with queries from above (limit 10-15 results).
3. For any promising URL, use `web_extract` to get details.
4. Deduplicate by company + title across sites.
5. Format and present the best 5-7 results in French.

### When to flag something to Baptiste:
- Role matches PE/HF/M&A in Paris or London with a Jan 2027 start
- Off-cycle internship at a BB or known PE firm
- Salary data above market
- "Easy apply" or fast application processes
- Roles with deadlines approaching

### When NOT to flag:
- Internships starting before July 2026 (probably too early for Baptiste)
- Graduate programs requiring 0-2 years experience that started recruiting in fall 2025
- Sales/trading or non-target roles
